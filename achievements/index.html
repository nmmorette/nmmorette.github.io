Awards

Top-Rated Instructor — Hackers do Bem, Wave 1 Issued by RNP (Rede Nacional de Ensino e Pesquisa), December 2024.

Hackers do Bem is a Brazilian national cybersecurity training initiative that identifies and develops new talent across the country, offering free education in ethical hacking and offensive and defensive security. At the end of each training cycle, students vote to recognise outstanding instructors on teaching quality, engagement, technical knowledge, and the ability to inspire and support learners.

Letter of Recognition — Funpresp-Jud Issued by Funpresp-Jud, February 2026.

Formal letter from the Fundação de Previdência Complementar do Servidor Público Federal do Poder Judiciário (the complementary pension foundation for Brazil’s federal judiciary civil servants), signed by its Director, recognising my contribution as an independent security researcher for the responsible disclosure of several vulnerabilities identified in the foundation’s systems.

The research was carried out together with the CVE Hunters team: Marcelo Queiroz, Itauan Santos, Camila S. Andrade, Leandro Cavalcante, Michel Lazzarin and Jairo W.

Letters of Appreciation — NASA (×2) Issued by the NASA Office of the Chief Information Officer, February 2026.

Two letters from NASA’s Vulnerability Disclosure Policy (VDP) program, signed by the Acting Senior Agency Information Security Officer, recognising the identification and responsible reporting of vulnerabilities in NASA systems. Both reports were submitted through the agency’s VDP on Bugcrowd.

One of the two letters was issued jointly with fellow researcher Tr0p.
